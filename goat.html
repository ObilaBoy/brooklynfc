😢 CHAPTER 1: THE POOR KID FROM MADEIRA (1985-2002)

Born: 5 February 1985, Funchal, Madeira, Portugal.

Cristiano was NOT born rich. His family was POOR.
• Father: José Dinis Aveiro — a gardener + part-time kit man at a local club. He earned almost nothing.
• Mother: Maria Dolores — a cook. She worked all day to feed 4 children.
• House: A tiny tin-roof shack in Santo António. No money. Sometimes they had no food. They shared one small room.
• His Name: His father named him "Ronaldo" after his favourite actor — US President Ronald Reagan!

The Pain: Other kids laughed at him. He had a Madeira accent. He was skinny. He cried when he missed his family.

At Age 12: Sporting Lisbon wanted him. But it meant leaving home — flying 1 hour away to Lisbon, alone, at 12 years old. He cried every night. He had no money for phone calls. He was homesick and bullied.

At Age 15: Doctors found his heart was beating too fast — Racing Heart / Tachycardia. He could have DIED. He had laser surgery on his heart. He was back training DAYS later. Most kids would quit. He said: "I am not scared. I will be the best."

His Secret: While others went home, Cristiano stayed and trained alone. Extra sprints. Extra free kicks. Extra weights. He said: "Your love makes me strong. Your hate makes me unstoppable."
