😢 CHAPTER 1: THE POOR KID FROM MADEIRA (1985-2002)

Born: 5 February 1985, Funchal, Madeira, Portugal.

Cristiano was NOT born rich. His family was POOR.
• Father: José Dinis Aveiro — a gardener + part-time kit man at a local club. He earned almost nothing.
• Mother: Maria Dolores — a cook. She worked all day to feed 4 children.
• House: A tiny tin-roof shack in Santo António. No money. Sometimes they had no food. They shared one small room.
• His Name: His father named him "Ronaldo" after his favourite actor — US President Ronald Reagan!

The Pain: Other kids laughed at him. He had a Madeira accent. He was skinny. He cried when he missed his family.

At Age 12: Sporting Lisbon wanted him. But it meant leaving home — flying 1 hour away to Lisbon, alone, at 12 years old. He cried every night. He had no money for phone calls. He was homesick and bullied.

At Age 15: Doctors found his heart was beating too fast — Racing Heart / Tachycardia. He could have DIED. He had laser surgery on his heart. He was back training DAYS later. Most kids would quit. He said: "I am not scared. I will be the best."

His Secret: While others went home, Cristiano stayed and trained alone. Extra sprints. Extra free kicks. Extra weights. He said: "Your love makes me strong. Your hate makes me unstoppable."

🔴 CHAPTER 2: SPORTING LISBON (2002-2003) - The Boy Who Destroyed Man United

Age: 17-18 • Games: 31 • Goals: 5

STRUGGLE: He was just a skinny kid with stepovers. Coaches said he was too selfish, too show-off, too skinny.

SUCCESS: On 14 August 2003, Sporting played a friendly vs Manchester United. Ronaldo DESTROYED them. He dribbled past everyone. United players were SHOCKED. After the game, United stars begged Sir Alex Ferguson: "SIGN HIM! SIGN HIM NOW!"

Transfer: Man United paid £12.24 MILLION — the most ever for a teenager in England at the time!

🔴 CHAPTER 3: MANCHESTER UNITED (2003-2009) - From Show-Off To Superstar

Age: 18-24 • Games: 292 • Goals: 118 • Trophies: 10

STRUGGLES:
• Given No.7 shirt — the shirt of George Best, Eric Cantona, David Beckham. Huge pressure! Fans said "He is not ready!"
• First 2 years: He did too many stepovers, no end product. English media called him a diver, a crybaby.
• 2004: His father DIED from alcohol problems at age 52. Ronaldo was broken. He couldn't go to the funeral properly. He cried on the pitch.
• 2006 World Cup: He winked after Rooney was sent off. ALL of England HATED him. Fans booed him every game.

HOW HE CONQUERED:
He changed his body. He trained like a monster. He stopped show-off tricks and started scoring. In 2007-08 he scored 42 GOALS as a winger — unheard of!

SUCCESS:
🏆 3 Premier Leagues (2007, 2008, 2009)
🏆 1 Champions League (2008 - Beat Chelsea on penalties in Moscow)
🏆 1 FIFA Club World Cup, 1 FA Cup, 2 League Cups
👑 1st Ballon d'Or (2008) — First United player since George Best in 1968!

He left as a KING. United fans still sing his name.

HOW HE CONQUERED:
He became a MACHINE. He scored in EVERY Clásico. He scored HAT-TRICKS for fun. He broke every record:

SUCCESS:
