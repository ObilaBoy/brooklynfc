📜 REAL MADRID HISTORY

Founded 6 March 1902. From a small Madrid club to Best Club of the 20th Century...

📖 Read More - Full History

1902-1950s: Founded as Madrid Football Club. King Alfonso XIII gave title "Real" (Royal) in 1920 — that's why crown on crest! Won first Copa del Rey 1905. Club almost died in Spanish Civil War. Santiago Bernabéu became president 1943 and REBUILT everything — built stadium, built team.

1950s - Kings of Europe: Bernabéu signed Di Stéfano, Puskás, Gento. Won FIRST 5 European Cups in a row! 1956, 1957, 1958, 1959, 1960. No one has ever done that again.

1960s-80s: Dominated Spain. "Quinta del Buitre" — Butragueño, Sanchís, Michel — won 5 La Ligas in a row 1986-1990.

2000s - Galácticos: Florentino signed Figo, Zidane, Ronaldo, Beckham. Won La Décima (10th UCL) in 2014 — Ramos 93rd minute header vs Atlético!

2016-2018: Under Zidane, won UCL 3 YEARS IN A ROW! 2016, 2017, 2018. Only club in modern era.

2024 - La Decimoquinta: 15th UCL! Beat Dortmund 2-0 at Wembley. Carvajal + Vini scored. First club to win 100 trophies!

2026: Mourinho is BACK! New era with Mbappé, Bellingham, Vini, Trent, Huijsen. Dream is La Decimosexta — 16th UCL!
