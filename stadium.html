📖 Read More - Full Stadium History
1947 - BORN: After Spanish Civil War, Real Madrid had no stadium. Bernabéu said "We will build the biggest in Spain!" He opened Nuevo Estadio Chamartín on 14 Dec 1947 — Madrid beat Belenenses 3-1. First goal by Sabino Barinaga.

1955 - RENAMED: Stadium renamed Santiago Bernabéu to honour the president who built it.

1957 - FIRST UCL FINAL: Hosted first European Cup Final — Madrid beat Fiorentina 2-0 in front of 124,000 fans! Still a record crowd!

1982 - WORLD CUP FINAL: Italy beat West Germany 3-1 here. The whole world watched Bernabéu.

2010 - UCL FINAL: Inter (Mourinho!) beat Bayern 2-0 here. Mourinho won his 2nd UCL at Bernabéu.

2019-2024 - NEW BERNABÉU: Florentino rebuilt it completely! New silver wrap, retractable roof that closes in 15 minutes, retractable pitch, 360° video screen, new museum, restaurants, shops. Now it hosts NFL games, Taylor Swift concerts, everything! It is not just a stadium — it is a city inside a stadium!
